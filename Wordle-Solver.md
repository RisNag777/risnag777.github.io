I am part of a family of Wordle-solvers. Despite falling off the Wordle wagon over the last year, it's still a part of my life. My wife, brother, parents all religiously play Wordle and love to maintain their daily streaks! So, it felt like a good idea to attempt to build an Agentic Wordle solver!  
My goal was to have a system similar to Wordle's own WordleBot, an algorithm that rates your performance and compares your guesses with the guesses that it would have made.  
WordleBot's guesses are typically words that would eliminate the largest number of possible solutions from the remaining solution-space. My idea was to instead, use an agent to select the next guess.
